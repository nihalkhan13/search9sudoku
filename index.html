How to play Search Nine

  1. Normal Sudoku: fill every row, column and 3×3 box with the digits 1–9, no repeats.
  2. Arrows: a cell with a grey arrow points toward the digit 9 in that row (left/right arrows) or column (up/down arrows).
  3. Distance: the digit you write in an arrow cell is exactly how many cells away that 9 is.

Example: an arrow pointing right holding a 3 means the 9 of that row sits exactly three cells to its right. An arrow cell can never contain a 9, since the distance would be zero.

Controls

  • Click, or click-and-drag, to select cells. Shift or Ctrl extends the selection.
  • 1–9 enters a digit · Shift+digit for corner marks · Ctrl+digit for centre marks.
  • Z X C V switch entry mode · Space cycles.
  • Backspace clears · Ctrl+Z undo · Esc deselects.
  • H hides or shows your cell colours (they are kept, not erased) · D toggles dark mode.
